## 2. What this reference contributes

This deck provides a strong corporate storytelling grammar that is complementary to evidence-heavy scientific/board decks:

- very generous white space;
- one dominant message per slide;
- large, direct titles and lighter gray explanatory subtitles;
- strong clover-green navigation and emphasis;
- large symbolic photography used as a narrative anchor rather than decoration;
- curved/organic image masks on hero and section slides;
- large process cards, arrows and stepped flows rather than dense prose;
- clear visual separation of "what is known", "what happens next", roles, decisions and responsibilities;
- disciplined footer with EUROIMMUN/From Revvity identity, confidentiality and page number.

## 3. Corporate-storytelling mode

Use this grammar when the presentation objective is primarily:

- internal alignment;
- transformation/change communication;
- town hall;
- leadership briefing;
- people/organization/process communication;
- orientation, rollout or implementation communication.

Do not force this grammar onto highly technical/scientific slides where evidence density, plots, references and analytical comparison require a different layout.

## 4. Preferred slide archetypes

### Hero / cover

- large title block with generous breathing room;
- short subtitle/tagline;
- one strong image or abstract brand visual, often occupying a large corner/side region;
- avoid decorative collections of icons.

### Agenda / orientation

- 3–5 concise topics maximum;
- left-aligned text hierarchy;
- optional chevron/arrow cue;
- one large symbolic image on the opposite side.

### Section divider

- large clover/brand field with white section title;
- large photo on the opposing side;
- soft/curved visual boundary between color field and image.

### Statement + evidence

- conclusion-led title;
- one key number, message or short sequence;
- one supporting diagram/image;
- optional pale-green conclusion strip near bottom.

### Process / implementation

- horizontal sequence or staged columns;
- numbered steps with repeated geometry;
- explicit outcome/result at each step where useful;
- directional arrows are functional, not decorative.

### Roles / responsibilities

- parallel role cards or phases;
- one icon/visual anchor per role where useful;
- verb-led responsibility text;
- strong contrast between current/future/transition states.

### Do / avoid

- balanced two-column structure;
- explicit positive/negative markers;
- paired language and geometry;
- every prohibition paired with a constructive alternative where possible.

### Timeline

- months/time points clearly anchored;
- multiple workstreams may run in parallel;
- labels and effective dates are visible without reading notes;
- minimize decorative styling around the actual timing logic.

### Summary / next steps

- 3–5 key messages;
- one explicit leadership/decision implication;
- end with a clear next action rather than a generic closing statement.

## 5. Visual grammar

### Composition

- keep large unused white areas intentionally;
- prefer one dominant visual anchor over multiple equal-weight objects;
- use cards only where they communicate a process, role, state or grouping;
- repeated structures should align to a clear baseline/grid;
- use pale tinted bands/surfaces for synthesis and takeaways, not as decorative backgrounds.

### Photography

- use realistic, high-quality photography with a clear metaphor or communication purpose;
- use one strong photo rather than mosaics when possible;
- curved/organic image boundaries are permitted for corporate storytelling and section navigation;
- do not distort scientific or evidentiary images to match these masks.

### Icons

When the runtime EUROIMMUN icon bundle is available, follow `docs/corporate/euroimmun/ICON_SYSTEM.md`.

- icons support labels/processes; they do not replace the message;
- use one family/variant consistently within a visual structure;
- clover icons are preferred on white backgrounds for positive/navigational emphasis;
- black icons are preferred for neutral/technical structures;
- circular white-on-clover/black variants are reserved for deliberate badge treatment.

### Color

- clover/forest green carry brand/navigation/positive action;
- black/dark gray carry primary information;
- medium gray carries explanatory secondary text;
- other palette colors are semantic accents only and should not compete with the main green hierarchy.

## 6. Typography rule

The active task template remains authoritative for font family. The storytelling reference demonstrates **hierarchy and scale**, not a universal Arial mandate.

- large, direct titles;
- secondary explanatory subtitle can be lighter gray;
- short content blocks rather than long paragraphs;
- section titles can be very large in white on the color field;
- never shrink body copy to preserve a layout that should instead be split.

## 7. Interaction with scientific/board decks

A corporate NDD/IVD strategy deck may combine both grammars:

- use the active scientific/management template for evidence, regulatory, clinical, competitive and financial slides;
- use corporate-storytelling archetypes for cover, agenda, section dividers, roadmap, decision, portfolio, roles, next steps and executive summaries;
- do not turn scientific evidence slides into lifestyle/photo slides;
- do not turn leadership/decision slides into dense report tables.

The result should feel like one EUROIMMUN deck, not two templates pasted together. Theme, footer, master and active binary template always remain authoritative.

## 8. QA additions

For a deck using corporate-storytelling mode, final QA must additionally check:

- every slide has one dominant message;
- image is informational/metaphorical rather than generic decoration;
- process cards/arrows are semantically directional;
- repeated cards use consistent geometry, spacing and icon sizing;
- section dividers are visually distinct from analytical content slides;
- green accent remains disciplined and does not become a background on every slide;
- titles/subtitles create a clear 2-level hierarchy;
- no dense report prose has been forced into a storytelling layout;
- no storytelling visual hides scientific limitations, caveats or sources.

## 9. Completion rule

A corporate-storytelling deck is successful when the message sequence can be understood from the visible slides without speaker notes, the deck remains visually restrained and recognizably EUROIMMUN, and the active template/brand/footer contract remains intact.
